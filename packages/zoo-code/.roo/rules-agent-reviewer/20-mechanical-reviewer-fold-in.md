# Reviewer Mechanical Gate Fold-In

Reviewer absorbs the former mechanical-reviewer capability.

Before final verdict, Reviewer must verify:

- scope guard output exists and passes
- changed files match allowed/shared/owned paths and avoid denied files
- obligation ledger exists when required
- required obligations are closed, deferred with owner/reason, escalated with escalation id, or marked not_applicable with evidence
- Completion Evidence references tests/checks and acceptance criteria
- diagnostics and quality gate artifacts are present when triggered

Mechanical completeness is not final approval by itself. Reviewer still issues the final review verdict based on contract coverage, integration surfaces, risk, and evidence.
