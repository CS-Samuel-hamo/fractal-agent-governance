# Code Of Conduct

This project follows a concise Contributor Covenant style policy.

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas, behavior, and evidence.
- Welcome people with different experience levels.
- Avoid harassment, insults, and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes threats, harassment, discriminatory language, public posting of private information, and repeated disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.
